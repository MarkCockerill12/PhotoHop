# A tour of the window

PhotoHop's window has six areas. You can resize the side panels and the timeline by dragging their edges.

| Area | Where | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Menu bar | very top | File, Edit, Layer, AI, View and Help menus |
| Toolbar | under the menus | the tools (Move, Select, Crop, Brush, Eraser, Pen, Hand) and the current tool's options |
| Layers | left | every layer in the project; top of the list = top of the picture |
| Canvas | middle | your picture; zoom with the mouse wheel |
| Inspector | right | settings of the selected layer, in numbered steps |
| Timeline | bottom | time, play/pause and keyframes (mostly for video) |
| Status bar | very bottom | what just happened, hints and progress bars |

## The menu bar

- **File** — new, open, save, import, export, and the Blender round trip. See [Menus](menus#file).
- **Edit** — undo and redo (everything can be undone, including brush strokes).
- **Layer** — add, duplicate and delete layers, plus *Remove background*, *Split into separate objects* and *One-click fit*.
- **AI** — all the AI tools, grouped into Photos, Video and For Blender. See [AI tools](ai).
- **View** — scopes, zoom and video preview quality.
- **Help** — this manual, shortcuts and the browser version.

## The toolbar

Click a tool or press its key. The right part of the toolbar shows options for the current tool (brush size, selection shape and so on). See [Tools](tools).

## The Layers panel

Each row has an eye (show/hide), a lock, a small icon for the kind of layer (photo, video, paint, colour) and the layer's name. Click a name to select it. The arrows on the right move a layer up or down. The buttons at the top add layers. See [Layers](layers).

## The canvas

Shows the result. Scroll to zoom towards the mouse pointer; drag with the **middle mouse button**, or hold **Space** and drag, to pan. **Ctrl+0** fits the whole picture in the window.

## The inspector

When a layer is selected, the inspector shows its settings as collapsible sections. For the bottom (background) layer you only see the sections that make sense for a background. With nothing selected, it shows the **Canvas** settings: the picture size, frame rate, length and background colour. Hover over any slider name to see what it does.

## The timeline

Play/pause, step frame by frame, and see each layer as a bar in time. Diamond (◆) markers are keyframes. See [Timing & animation](timing).

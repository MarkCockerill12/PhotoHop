# Welcome to PhotoHop

PhotoHop puts you (or anything else) into another place — a game level, a wallpaper video, a Blender render or a real photo — and makes it look like you were really there. It is a small photo **and** video editor built for that one job: cut out, place, then match colour, light, shadows, grain and lens so everything reads as one picture.

## The idea in one minute

A PhotoHop project is a stack of **layers**, like sheets of glass on top of each other. The bottom layer is the **background** (the place). Layers on top are the things you add (you, a prop, a character). Everything you do is non-destructive: your original files are never changed, and every step can be undone with **Ctrl+Z**.

For each layer you add, the inspector on the right shows **numbered steps**. Work down them:

1. **Cut out** — keep only you, hide the rest of your photo. See [Cut out](cutout).
2. **Position & size** — drag yourself into place. See [Position & size](position).
3. **Fit into the scene** — one click matches colours, adds a shadow and grain. See [Fit into the scene](fit).
4. **Colour adjustments** — fine-tune by hand if you want. See [Colour adjustments](colour).
5. **Shadow & light** — adjust the shadow on the ground and the light direction. See [Shadow & light](shadow).
6. **Camera & lens look** — grain, blur, haze and glow so you look shot with the same camera. See [Camera & lens look](camera).

Then **File > Export** saves the finished picture or video. See [Saving & exporting](export).

> New here? Follow [Your first composite](first-composite) — it walks through a whole picture in about five minutes.

## Finding your way around this manual

- The **contents** on the left are grouped from beginner to reference.
- **Search** (the box at the top) finds every section that mentions all the words you type. Results show the page and section; click one to jump there.
- Every section in the app has a **? Help** button that opens the matching page here.
- Press **F1** anywhere in PhotoHop to open or close the manual.
- **Open in web browser** shows the same manual as one web page you can keep, print or read on another screen. The release download also includes it as `PhotoHop-Manual.html`.

## What PhotoHop will and won't do with AI

AI is optional. When it is used, it only makes **masks** (which pixels belong to you) and **settings** (colour and light values you can see and change). It never repaints your picture. The single exception is [Remove an object](ai#remove-an-object), which paints only inside the object you asked to remove, on a separate layer you can hide. See [AI tools](ai).

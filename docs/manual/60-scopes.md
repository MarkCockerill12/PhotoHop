# Scopes

Scopes measure the picture so you don't have to trust your eyes (which adapt to colour casts). Press **S** or click **Scopes** at the right of the toolbar.

| Scope | Shows | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| Waveform | brightness of every column of the picture, left to right | compare the black and white levels of you and the background |
| Parade | the waveform separately for red, green and blue | spot colour casts: neutral greys have equal R, G and B |
| Vectorscope | colour (hue = angle, saturation = distance from centre), with a skin-tone line | check skin tones lie on the line; compare saturation |
| Histogram | how many pixels have each brightness | check clipping (piled up at either end) |

Black lands on the bottom row and white on the top row of the waveform and parade.

> Matching trick: put your layer next to something neutral in the scene (grey concrete, a white wall) and compare their heights in the parade.
